Why one office beats a roster of vendors

Strategy, marketing, design and development fail at the seams between them. A single accountable team removes the seams.

AuthorWalter Lentfert

Most enterprise web programs are split across a strategy consultant, a creative agency, a development shop and a marketing-ops contractor. Each is competent. The trouble is what happens between them.1

Every hand-off is a translation. Intent gets compressed into a brief, the brief into a design, the design into tickets. By launch, the original business challenge is three documents away.

The work fails at the seams, so remove the seams.

One office changes the unit of accountability. The person who heard the problem is in the room when it is built, and still answers the phone after launch.2
