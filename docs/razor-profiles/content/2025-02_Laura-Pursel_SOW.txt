Scope of Work
Summary
Laura Pursel will create a visual design for the Razor Profiles website with opportunities for
additional visual exploration/brand expansion.


Existing assets include a site map, module library, and brand guidelines will be enhanced to
create a comprehensive guideline for the use of the brand when applied to digital assets.


Razor Profiles will be responsible for the delivery of the final sitemap, wireframes and User
Flow, with content element direction as required and prioritized for each page.


The final deliverable will include high-fidelity site mockups, design reviews and refinements,
and development-ready assets created in collaboration and communication with the site
developer.



Timeline
The design phase of the project (Phase 1 – 3) will be delivered in 8 weeks using Figma for all
design elements to and Asana as our project management and documentation tool.



Phase 1 – Discovery & Alignment

   •   Review existing brand guidelines and identify key pieces of visual strategy to
       explore and/or expand upon
   •   Define and discuss potential key page moments, user flow points, and priority
       features/messaging
   •   Razor Profiles to provide access to wireframes and userflow for identified personae
   •   Moodboard initial visual direction concepts to present and revise for approval


Deliverables: 2 key messaging and visual strategy exercises and 1 wireframe/user flow
