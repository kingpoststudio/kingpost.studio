Razor Profiles Design Estimate​
Laura Pursel www.laurapursel.co​
February 2025​
​
Scope of Work
Summary: ​
Visual design for Razor Profiles website with opportunities for additional visual exploration/brand expansion.
Existing assets include a site map, module library, and brand guidelines. The final deliverable will include
high-fidelity site mockups, design reviews and refinements, and development-ready assets created in
collaboration and communication with the site developer.

Services/Role: Visual and brand designer for website development​
Hourly Rate: $90/hr​
Estimated Timeline: 12 Weeks​



Phase 1 – Discovery & Alignment (Week 1–2)

    ●​   Review existing brand guidelines and identify key pieces of visual strategy to explore and/or expand
         upon
    ●​   Define and discuss potential key page moments, user flow points, and priority features/messaging
    ●​   Moodboard initial visual direction concepts to present and revise for approval

Deliverables: 2-3 key messaging and visual strategy exercises, 3-4 moodboard directions for overall website
look, feel, and ideas for key features or moments​
Estimated Hours: 10–15 hours​
Estimated Cost: $900 – $1,350

​
Phase 2 – High-Fidelity Design (Week 3–8)

Core Page Designs:

    ●​   Homepage
         ○​ hero visuals, navigation, primary call-to-action sections
    ●​   About Us
         ○​ company story, team section styling
    ●​   Services Overview & Subpages
         ○​ Digital Strategy, Website Development, CRM, etc.
    ●​   Contact Page
         ○​ form UI, contact details layout
    ●​   Marketing Agencies & Other Experiences
         ○​ visual distinctions per category
    ●​   Privacy Policy & Terms Pages
         ○​ minimal styling, brand-aligned typography
Deliverables: 6 high fidelity designs with content modules from existing library and new considerations for
additions. Annotated notes for ideas in development and design opportunities included in presenting/sharing
documents.

Estimated Hours: 55–70 hours​
Estimated Cost: $4,950 – $6,300

​
Phase 3 – Additional Visual Exploration (Week 6–9)

    ●​        UI elements exploration and review (buttons, navigation, content block consistency refinements)
    ●​        Iconography, illustration, and art/photography sourcing, if needed
    ●​        Identifying moments for potential animation/motion graphics and scoping development

Estimated Hours: 20–30 hours​
Estimated Cost: $1,800 – $2,700

​
Phase 4 – Design Finalization & Handoff (Week 10–12)

    ●​        Reviews, refinements & consistency checks
    ●​        Preparing development-ready assets (Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD, or required format)
    ●​        Design QA support post-handoff (up to 5 hours)

Estimated Hours: 20–25 hours​
Estimated Cost: $1,800 – $2,250

​

Total Estimated Hours & Cost
         ●​     Total Estimated Hours: 105–140 hours
         ●​     Total Estimated Cost: $9,450 – $12,600

​
