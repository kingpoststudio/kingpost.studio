Clean Cut

The mark’s three swooshes become three flat bands separated by razor-thin cuts. Accuracy, speed and delight are the bands; taste is the cut, what we leave out. A bright, flat, cut-paper system: big colour planes, one chamfered corner, no gradients, no mono labels.

The mark: an evolution, not a replacement

Same silhouette and the same Philosopher wordmark paths from the live SVG. What changes: the raster gradient becomes three flat vector bands, cut at the three original overlap points.
